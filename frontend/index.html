About

Twelve years on the same corner

CafeBot started as a two-person cart and never quite outgrew the habits that came with it: buy small, make it to order, know the regulars by their drink.

Beans come from a single roaster three streets over, and we cup every lot before it goes on bar. Milk and eggs come from a farm co-op an hour outside the city. Whatever is left at close goes to the shelter on Ninth, never the bin.

If the queue is long, the coffee is worth the wait. If it isn’t, tell us and we’ll make it again.

— Rosa Delgado, owner
